01 · Overview

What this is

DropIn AI Editor turns one long recording (a Q&A, podcast, interview or talk) into a set of vertical clips that are ready to post. It transcribes the footage, finds the strongest moments, cuts them cleanly, reframes to 9:16 around the speaker's face, burns in word-by-word captions, lays music under the voice, and checks its own work before anything is called done.

There is no app to log into. It is a set of skills that run inside Claude Code on your machine, driving a short list of proven tools. Your Claude Code session is the editor: it reads the bundled editorial playbooks and makes the cut and caption decisions itself, so you never need a separate model API key.

03 · The workflow

How a clip gets made

You give one instruction and the kit walks this path. The tags mark where your taste comes in.

> /edit "cut this Q&A into 6 vertical clips"
  1. 01Set up the project. Copy the footage from a file, link or drive into a clean folder: source, work, deliver. Originals are never touched.
  2. 02Read the footage. Find faces, camera angles and audio levels so later steps frame and cut correctly.
  3. 03Transcribe. Word-level timings with speakers and fillers, the spine for every cut.
  4. 04Find the best moments. Score candidates against a real editorial rubric and rank them.your taste
  5. 05Lock the in and out. Each clip gets a hook, context and payoff, with no clipped words or false endings.
  6. 06Cut and clean. Remove filler, dead air and stumbles, joining on silences so it sounds natural.
  7. 07Reframe to vertical. Face-tracked 9:16 that keeps the speaker centered.your look
  8. 08Caption. Word-by-word captions in your font, colors and emphasis style.your style
  9. 09Add music. Match a track to each clip's energy and sit it under the voice.your music
  10. 10Render. Built in cached stages, so a later fix rebuilds only what changed.
  11. 11Check: six gates. Every gate must pass. A clip that fails one does not ship.
  12. 12Deliver. Finished clips land in the deliver folder for your final look, or go straight to Frame.io and Monday.

Revisions are targeted. Fix a caption typo and only the caption stage runs again, not the whole clip.

04 · Quality control

The six check gates

This is what makes it safe to run hands-off. After each render, six separate checks each look at one thing, without knowing how the clip was made. All six must pass.

Gate 1

Mechanics

Resolution, length, black or frozen frames, clean first and last frames.

Gate 2

Story

A real hook, one clear arc, and a payoff that lands on the last line.

Gate 3

Picture

Face tracking and framing hold, with no jarring jumps or artifacts.

Gate 4

Sound

No clipped words, pops or level jumps. Music sits under the voice.

Gate 5

Captions

Accurate words, tight timing, correct colors, emphasis and placement.

Gate 6

Script

Would a cold viewer follow it? Context arrives before the payoff.

It learns from you. When you give feedback on a clip, the fix becomes a standing rule, so the same mistake does not ship twice. That is why the output gets better each cycle instead of drifting.

05 · Make it yours

What you plug in

The kit is brand-neutral on purpose. These five inputs make the clips look and sound like you instead of a template.

1

Your footage

Drop raw recordings into the project's 00_SOURCE/ folder, or hand it a YouTube or drive link. The kit always copies first.

2

Your clip taste

What makes a moment worth clipping for your audience: your hooks, topics and the kind of payoff you end on. Write it once and every batch uses it.

3

Your caption look

Font, colors, emphasis and position. Free Montserrat ships as the default; drop your own font into brand/fonts/.

4

Your music

Point DROPIN_MUSIC at your royalty-free library. The kit matches a track to each clip's energy and avoids repeating one within a batch.

5

Your connections

AssemblyAI and Gemini keys, plus Groq for the audit gates. The agent walks you through each one and saves it privately on your machine, never in the repo or the chat.

All of it lives in plain text and config files. No code changes needed to make it yours.

06 · Get running

Install and run

What you need

Claude Code, Python 3.10+, ffmpeg with libass, and tesseract. yt-dlp and rclone for link and drive ingest; Node only for promos.

1 · Get the kit

git clone https://github.com/Louiefigz/dropin-editing
cd dropin-editing && ./setup.sh

2 · Let the agent set you up

Open the folder in Claude Code and say "Set up DropIn AI Editor for me." It connects your keys, asks about your brand and makes a test clip.

3 · Make clips

/edit <your youtube link>

Finished, checked clips land in the project's 20_DELIVER/ folder.

Check readiness

python3 plugins/dropin-ai-editor/doctor.py

First run

Test one short clip end to end to confirm ffmpeg, your keys and your assets are wired up. After that, batches just work.
